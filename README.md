# AI Engineering Journey

> A structured, project-based journey to becoming an AI Systems Engineer with strong fundamentals in Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, and Production AI Systems.

---

## 📌 About

This repository serves as the central roadmap for my AI Engineering journey.

Instead of following traditional tutorial-based learning, this journey focuses on understanding concepts from first principles and immediately applying them through hands-on implementation, real-world projects, and production-oriented engineering practices.

The objective is to build deep technical knowledge, strong problem-solving skills, and the ability to independently design, build, deploy, and scale AI systems.

---

## 🎯 Goals

- Build strong AI fundamentals from scratch.
- Understand the mathematics behind AI.
- Implement important algorithms from scratch.
- Build production-ready AI applications.
- Learn modern AI technologies and industry practices.
- Develop strong software engineering skills.
- Build a professional portfolio through real projects.
- Prepare for top AI engineering roles and M.Tech programs.
- Contribute to open-source projects.
- Build AI products and eventually an AI startup.

---

## 🧠 Learning Philosophy

Every concept in this journey follows the same learning framework:

```
Problem
    ↓
Theory
    ↓
Mathematics
    ↓
Implementation
    ↓
Mini Project
    ↓
Industry Usage
    ↓
Deployment
    ↓
Reflection
```

The focus is not on completing courses but on developing deep understanding through continuous building and experimentation.
